# Curriculum content policy

Canonical syllabus: https://machinelearning-club.github.io/

The app supports 15 weekly sessions rather than reproducing the entire website. Every week retains four distinct categories:

- **Course:** self-contained, concise conceptual text adapted to pre-university learners; deep reading stays on the web.
- **Laboratory:** visual first, executable demonstrations / links to Colab.
- **Homework:** concise tasks and explicit expected deliverables.
- **Advanced:** optional extension for students who want technical depth.

## Content-release gates

Every available in-app activity must include an age-appropriate objective, examples, testable expected result, explanatory feedback and accessible wording. Do not label incomplete lessons as published. Date/content review should occur when the website syllabus changes.

**Week 1:** rules, classification, regression, clustering, search/planning, reinforcement learning, generative AI. One seven-scenario matching quiz, with explanatory correction.

**Weeks 2–4:** data, statistics, decision trees and evaluation. Interactive maths/math visualisations before formal derivation.

**Weeks 5–9:** KNN, K-Means, linear regression, vision, perceptron/neural networks; visually controlled experiments.

**Weeks 10–12:** connected AI systems, forecasting and checking LLM outputs; safety and human verification.

**Weeks 13–15:** FIICode-style baseline, controlled model improvement and mock hackathon.

## Source use

[spantiru/companion-lab](https://github.com/spantiru/companion-lab) offers university notebooks (probability, trees, Naive Bayes, logistic regression, KNN and clustering), **not** source code for this Android app. Adapt ideas to the school level. Do not copy notebooks, illustrations, exercises, datasets, or third-party text without verifying rights and attribution.

The club website is the source of official session titles/dates; validate against its live syllabus before each content release.
